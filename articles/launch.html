這是什麼｜What this is

1974 年 11 月 16 日，阿雷西博天文台向 M13 球狀星團送出一則 1,679 位元的訊息。1,679 是兩個質數 23 和 73 的乘積，所以這串位元剛好可以排成 23 欄、73 列的圖。

On November 16, 1974, the Arecibo Observatory sent a 1,679-bit message toward the globular cluster M13. 1,679 is the product of two primes, 23 and 73, so the bits fit exactly into a 23-by-73 picture.

ARECIBO Signal 把這個點子搬進麥田：壓倒的麥子是 1，站著的是 0。你在 arecibosignal.com 輸入一句話，就會得到一個真的能解碼的麥田圈，可以下載 PNG。全部在你的瀏覽器裡完成；挑戰模式的分享連結只帶位元，不帶你的原文。

ARECIBO Signal moves that idea into a wheat field: pressed crop is 1, standing crop is 0. Type a sentence on arecibosignal.com and you get a crop circle that really decodes, ready to download as a PNG. Everything runs in your browser. Share links in challenge mode carry only the bits, never your text.
