**如何自己解碼一則「麥田圈訊息」**
2001年8月，英國奇爾伯頓（Chilbolton）電波望遠鏡旁出現一個麥田圖案，外觀模仿1974年的阿雷西博訊息，被暱稱為「回信」。普遍認為它是人為製作，但它正好是練習解碼的好教材：
1. **數格子**：把圖案看成一格一格的點，有點記1、沒點記0。原始阿雷西博訊息共1679個位元。
2. **找質因數**：1679 = 23 × 73，只有這兩種排法。排成73列、每列23格，圖案才會「對齊」成畫面。
3. **由上往下讀**：依序是數字1–10、構成DNA的元素、DNA雙螺旋、人形、太陽系、發送天線。
4. **比對差異**：把麥田圖案和1974原版逐列比較，看哪幾列被改了（例如人形、元素、天線形狀），推敲改動想表達什麼。

**How to decode a "crop circle message" yourself**
In August 2001, a crop formation appeared next to the Chilbolton radio telescope in England, styled after the 1974 Arecibo message and nicknamed the "reply." It is widely considered human-made, but it makes a great decoding exercise:
1. **Count the cells**: treat the pattern as a grid; a dot is 1, a blank is 0. The original Arecibo message has 1,679 bits.
2. **Factor it**: 1679 = 23 × 73, the only two ways to arrange it. Lay it out as 73 rows of 23 cells and the picture lines up.
3. **Read top to bottom**: numbers 1–10, the elements in DNA, the double helix, a human figure, the solar system, and the transmitting dish.
4. **Spot the differences**: compare the crop pattern with the 1974 original row by row, find which rows changed (figure, elements, dish), and guess what the changes are trying to say.
